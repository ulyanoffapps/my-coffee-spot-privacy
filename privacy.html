My Coffee Spot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

For privacy questions, support or reports, email appsitetown@gmail.com. Our support page also provides this contact.

Information used by the game

  • Café and profile: Your chosen display name, café name, city and country, café design, menu and game details are stored in the public CloudKit database and can be accessible to other users.
  • Activity: Orders made with virtual coins, likes and stickers can include café identifiers, display names, countries, items, prices and times. Public CloudKit records may be accessible even when the app does not show them on every screen.
  • Progress and settings: Game progress, coins, passport, stickers, blocked cafés and preferences are saved on your device and may be saved in your private iCloud database.
  • iCloud identifier: CloudKit supplies an app-specific user record identifier. The game uses it to associate your account with your café and activity; some record identifiers are derived from it.
  • Location: If you choose to find your city and grant permission, the app obtains your location and uses Apple’s geocoding service. It stores and shares the chosen city, country, time zone and coordinates for its area. If a city-centre lookup is unavailable, coordinates rounded to one decimal place may be used. You can choose a city manually without granting location permission.
  • Notifications: If enabled, the app uses device notification settings and CloudKit subscriptions to deliver game updates.
  • Emails and reports: When you send a support or report email, we receive your email address and the information you include. A report draft includes the café identifier, name, city and reason you select. Earlier TestFlight builds could submit reports to a public CloudKit database, where they may be accessible to other app users.

How information is used and shared

We use information to operate the game, display cafés, save progress, support visits and stickers, show city weather, send requested notifications, and respond to reports and support messages. Apple services used by the game include iCloud and CloudKit, Maps and geocoding, WeatherKit and Apple Push Notification service. Apple processes information under its own privacy policy.

The game does not use advertising or advertising tracking. Coins are virtual game currency, not real-money payments.

Retention and deletion

Game information is retained while needed to provide the game or until you request deletion. Use the deletion control in Settings to start deleting your game data. The app removes data it can access through your iCloud account, and related public records are processed separately. Automated cleanup normally waits at least 24 hours after the device completes its stage; interruptions or records needing review can take longer. The app checks for a cleanup confirmation before starting a new game. Contact us if deletion does not finish.

Deleting an order before the café owner collects its virtual coins can prevent those coins from being credited. Copies on other devices or in device backups may require separate attention. Older app versions or a delayed write can recreate old data; update the app on other devices and contact us if deleted data reappears.

The app may keep a small deletion marker in your private iCloud database and a matching marker on your device. The marker contains random request identifiers and deletion status; it helps prevent an older device from restoring a deleted game. It does not contain the deleted café or game progress.

We use GitHub Actions to process eligible CloudKit cleanup requests. GitHub provides the infrastructure for that processing. Requests use an app-specific iCloud identifier; we protect the credentials used for CloudKit access.

We keep support messages and reports only as long as needed to respond, address safety concerns or meet applicable obligations. Some information may need to be kept longer where required by law or justified for safety or disputes. Contact us with questions about deletion or retention.

Your choices

You can change location and notification permissions in iOS Settings and manage the game’s notification preferences. You can change your displayed names and block or report cafés. Older shared interactions may continue to show the name used when they were created until those records are removed.

Children

Parents and guardians can contact us with questions about a child’s data. We will respond to requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game or our practices change. The current policy will be available through the app’s privacy information.
